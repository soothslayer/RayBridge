Optional Kokoro answer voice

Download the local Kokoro voice model once, then select Kokoro in the iPhone app’s Setup screen. Answers stay on this Mac until the generated audio is sent to your iPhone.

The quantized model is about 300 MB. The first download can take several minutes. Apple speech remains available if Kokoro cannot run.

In the iPhone app, connect your glasses and start listening. Questions use a recent camera frame; answers are spoken through the glasses.

This version takes turns: listen, answer, listen again. It does not provide ChatGPT’s native live voice or continuous video understanding. Keep the iPhone app open and this Mac awake.
